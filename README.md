## Research and experience

**Project Intern, IIT Madras — Mobile Air Quality Intelligence**  
Developed a multimodal roadside PM2.5 pipeline combining mobile sensors, OPC particle-size bins, atmospheric products, meteorology, GPS/OSM context, and camera imagery. Worked with YOLO, SegFormer, ResNet50-GRU, tree-based models, whole-date validation, and a filter-sampling/XRF validation workflow.

**Signalling and Telecommunications Intern — South Western Railways / RVNL**  
Studied Kyosan Electronic Interlocking in a safety-critical environment, including command-to-field feedback paths, relay interfaces, interlocking logic, and failure-mode handling.
